How we work.

Learn it before you teach it

Everyone, including engineers and designers, takes the courses. If a question confuses us, it'll confuse a learner.

Ship the hard version

A multiple choice question is easy to build. A Bode plot you can draw with your thumb is worth the extra weeks.

Small, calm, and written down

Few meetings, long documents, no heroics. We keep on-call quiet and evenings free.

Respect the five minutes

Our learners give us five minutes a day. Every screen has to earn its place in them.
