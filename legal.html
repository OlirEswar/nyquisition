Privacy policy

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email and password (stored hashed).
  • Learning activity: the questions you see, your answers, how long you take, and the notes saved for you.
  • Device data: app version, operating system and crash reports, so we can fix bugs.

How we use it

To schedule your quizzes, generate your notes, answer your questions to Nyq, and improve the courses. Aggregated, de-identified data may be used in learning research we publish.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train models for anyone else.

Your choices

You can export or delete your account and all of its data from settings at any time. Deletion is complete within 30 days, including backups.

Job applicants

If you apply for a role, we keep your application for 12 months to consider you for other openings, then delete it. Ask us any time and we'll delete it sooner.

Terms of service

Using Nyquisition

You need to be at least 13 to make an account. Keep your password private, and don't use the service to break the law or harm others.

Subscriptions

Max renews automatically until you cancel. You can cancel any time from settings, and keep Max until the end of the period you've paid for. Free trials convert to a paid plan unless cancelled before they end.

Content

Our courses, characters and software belong to Nyquisition, Inc. Your notes and answers are yours; you give us permission to store and process them to run the service.

AI-generated material

Some explanations and questions are generated or adapted by AI and reviewed by our curriculum team. They can still contain mistakes. Please report anything that looks wrong from the lesson screen.

Changes

If we make material changes to these terms, we'll email you at least 30 days before they take effect.
