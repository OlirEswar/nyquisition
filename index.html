What level is this for?

Roughly the second year of an engineering degree and beyond. If you've seen calculus and differential equations, you're ready. Each course starts with a short placement quiz so you skip what you already know.

Can five minutes a day really work for material this dense?

Five minutes of the right questions beats an hour of re-reading. Spaced retrieval practice is one of the best-replicated findings in learning research. The hard part is picking the right question on the right day, and that's the part the AI does.

How is the AI different from asking a chatbot?

It knows your history. Every question, answer and saved note feeds a model of what you know, so explanations build on the way you've understood things before, and its quizzes aim at real gaps rather than generic ones.

Will it replace my course or textbook?

It's built to sit beside them. Lots of learners use it to stay on top of a class they're taking, or to keep material fresh after the exam.

Which platforms does it run on?

iPhone, Android and the web. Your streak and notes sync between all three.

Can I cancel Max any time?

Yes. Cancel from settings and you keep Max until the end of the billing period. Your notes stay in your account either way.
